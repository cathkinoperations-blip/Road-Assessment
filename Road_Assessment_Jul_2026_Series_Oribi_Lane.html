Oribi Lane Series

Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (~140m Scope) — Tier 3 Medium-High Priority

Key Observations & Issues

  • Extended 100m Oxidation Zone: Dry, coarse surface texture requiring surface enrichment before environmental water damage escalates.
  • Pop-Out Potholes & Sunken Patches: Small pinhole aggregate pop-outs and failing patch perimeters along the incline causing localized dips.
  • Widespread Block Cracking (25–35m): Section covered in a web-like map cracking network across the full lane width on the incline section.

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • Patch Milling & Leveling: Mill out and level depressed historical patches, re-compact underlying base, and seal all perimeter joints.
  • Crack Routing & Hot-Pour Sealing: Rout out and seal all active longitudinal and block crack networks across the incline corridor.
  • Full-Corridor Slurry Rejuvenation: Apply a continuous bitumen slurry seal over the full ~140m corridor to stop top-down wearing coat breakdown and lock in aggregate.
