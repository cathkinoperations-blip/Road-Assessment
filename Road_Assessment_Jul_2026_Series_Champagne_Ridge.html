Champagne Ridge Series

Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (280m Total Scope) — Priority 1 Critical

Key Observations & Issues

  • Extended Binder Oxidation & Failure (280m): Complete waterproofing loss and aggregate stripping across the entire 280-meter corridor scope.
  • Inner-Curve Shoulder Breakout: Shear stress and uncontained stormwater runoff causing outer shoulder crumbling and bank erosion along curves.
  • Saddle Zone Water Retention & Base Delamination: Dip sections accumulating runoff, resulting in sub-base softening and total breakdown of historic patches.

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • Cast Concrete Edge Beams: Continuous 300mm × 250mm reinforced concrete edge beams along inner curves and slope sections for structural shoulder containment.
  • G2 Sub-Base Excavation: Deep box-cut excavation of failed patch zones and saddle dips, backfilled with G2 aggregate and compacted to 98% Mod AASHTO.
  • Full-Width 80mm Paver Overlay: Continuous full-width installation of Class 40/8 80mm interlocking concrete pavers on a 25mm bedding sand layer across the 280m length.
