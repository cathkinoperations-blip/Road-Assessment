Dierama Lane Series

Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (~90m Scope) — Tier 3 Medium Priority

Key Observations & Issues

  • Binder Embrittlement & Oxidation: The asphalt wearing course shows extensive surface drying and binder degradation, resulting in longitudinal cracking over a combined ~90m stretch.
  • Localized Fatigue Webbing: Concentrated wheel-path stress has produced localized alligator cracking with early-stage aggregate ravelling and pinhole pop-outs.
  • Structural Integrity Intact: No deep sub-base shear failure or shoulder washouts observed; road bed remains structurally sound for cost-effective holding intervention.

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • High-Pressure Air Blow-Out: Thoroughly clean all active crack channels using high-pressure compressed air and mechanical wire brushing to clear debris and organic matter.
  • Hot-Pour Elastomeric Joint Sealing: Rout and inject rubberized hot-pour bitumen sealant along the 60m apron stretch and 30m centerline fracture to prevent water ingress.
  • Bitumen Mastic & Band Sealing: Apply fine-aggregate bitumen mastic over localized alligator crack zones and execute a 100mm wide over-band seal to bind loose aggregate.
