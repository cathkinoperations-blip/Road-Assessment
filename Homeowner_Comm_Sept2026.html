Cathkin Estates Homeowners Association

Pre-Read Memorandum: Road Infrastructure Strategy & Additional Funding Request
Date: September 2026 | Target Meeting: Upcoming Homeowner Special General Meeting

Executive Summary & Meeting Purpose

The HOA Board has completed a comprehensive site-wide road audit and tender evaluation. To shift from costly recurring patch repairs to a permanent, low-maintenance road network, we are adopting a Low-Total Cost of Ownership (Low-TCO) strategy using 80mm heavy-duty interlocking pavers and concrete curb containment.

Key Decision Required: Our remaining approved road budget is c. R480k. To execute Phase 1 (Champagne Ridge rebuild & immediate pre-rainy season drainage/holding repairs), we require additional funding approval of R1.015M (bringing the FY 25/26 spend to R1.495M total including a 15% contingency).

1. Strategic Shift: Low-TCO Permanent Rebuilds

Patch repairs have failed to address core sub-base erosion, binder oxidation, and edge breakouts across our road network. To protect estate asset values, all future critical interventions will replace asphalt with engineered 80mm interlocking pavers anchored by G2 aggregate sub-bases and concrete edge restraints. This strategy eliminates recurring bitumen failures and safely handles heavy construction traffic.

2. Priority Focus: Champagne Ridge (Phase 1)

Champagne Ridge (~280m / 810m²) represents our most critical risk due to severe binder oxidation, inner-curve shoulder breakout, and failing sub-bases.

Why Act Now?

Executing Champagne Ridge and stormwater drainage prior to the November–February rainy season prevents severe structural sub-base washouts that would exponentially increase repair costs next year. The remaining Tier 1 and Tier 2 bridges (Bridge 2, 4, 5, 6, 8 & Dam) are scheduled for post-rainy season execution in FY 26/27 (budgeted at R1.38M).
