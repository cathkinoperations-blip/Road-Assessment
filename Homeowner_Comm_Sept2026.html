Cathkin Estates Homeowners Association

Pre-Read Memorandum: Road Infrastructure Strategy & Multi-Year Funding Request
Date: September 2026 | Target Meeting: Special General Meeting (SGM)

Executive Summary & Core Decision Requested

The HOA Board has completed a site-wide engineering assessment to transition our road network from costly, temporary asphalt patching to a permanent Low-Total Cost of Ownership (Low-TCO) standard using 80mm heavy-duty interlocking pavers and concrete kerb containment.

Key Decision Required: With our next AGM scheduled for July 2027, the Board requires formal homeowner approval for the total 2-FY Road Rehabilitation Program (R2.85M total including a 15% contingency) at this upcoming SGM. With c. R480k remaining in our current approved budget, this requires authorizing an additional R1.015M for FY 25/26 (pre-rainy season Phase 1) and approving the FY 26/27 budget allocation of R1.38M to execute immediately following the rainy season.

1. Strategic Shift: Low-TCO Permanent Rebuilds

Repeated surface patching has proved ineffective against sub-base erosion, binder oxidation, and edge breakouts. To protect long-term estate value and avoid wasteful spending, all critical failure points will be rebuilt using engineered 80mm interlocking pavers anchored by G2 aggregate sub-bases and concrete edge restraints designed to withstand multi-year construction traffic.

2. Immediate Priority: Champagne Ridge (Phase 1)

Champagne Ridge (~280m / 810m²) is our highest risk failure zone due to severe sub-base delamination, inner-curve shoulder breakout, and binder failure.

Vendor Evaluation & Recommendation:

  • The fixed-price contract scope fully guarantees G2 sub-base undercutting, concrete edge kerb containment along inner curves, and an 80mm heavy-duty paver system backed by structural warranties.

Why Multi-Year Approval is Necessary Now

Securing approval for both financial years at this SGM ensures we can complete Champagne Ridge and stormwater drainage before the high-intensity rainfall period (Nov–Feb), preventing catastrophic sub-base washouts. It also empowers the Board to seamlessly procure materials and lock in civil contractors for Phase 2 execution immediately when dry weather returns in March 2027, rather than delaying critical work until after the July 2027 AGM.
