Appendix A1: Structural Sub-Base Strategy

Technical Reference — Base Work Scope

A critical distinction in the master assessment plan is separating surface-level waterproofing from full structural sub-base reconstruction. Surface treatments do not automatically require redoing the sub-base, preserving capital where structural integrity remains intact.

1. Surface Sealing Only

Sub-base Left Intact

  • Mechanism: Applied directly onto existing asphalt.
  • Purpose: Waterproofs the surface, seals micro-cracks, and prevents binder oxidation.
  • Applicable Conditions: Fine map cracking, surface weathering, pinhole loss.
  • Target Corridors: Duiker Ridge, Village 4 & 5, Bridge 3 & 7 approaches.

2. Targeted Box Cuts

Localized Base Repairs

  • Mechanism: Cold-milling or hand-excavating localized failure zones down to sub-base.
  • Purpose: Rebuilds isolated sub-base collapse before applying a continuous surface seal.
  • Applicable Conditions: Wheel-path alligator cracking, isolated depressions, sunken patches.
  • Target Corridors: Bridge 5, Eland Way, Oribi Lane.

3. Full Structural Rebuild

Full Depth Excavation / Paver Upgrade

  • Mechanism: Total removal of failed sub-base; installation of concrete edge beams, drainage, or pavers.
  • Purpose: Re-establishes structural load-bearing capacity, containment, and long-term durability.
  • Applicable Conditions: Severe washout trenches, edge scour, full corridor oxidation, high turning shear.
  • Target Corridors: Champagne Ridge (280m), Bridge 6 & 8 washout zones, Arum Lane apron.

Appendix A2: Bituminous Material Comparison

Technical Reference — Cape Seal vs. Slurry Overlay
Parameter Slurry Overlay (Slurry Seal) Cape Seal
System Construction Single-layer pre-mixture of bitumen emulsion, fine aggregate (sand/small stone), water, and cement filler. Two-step hybrid treatment: Single chip seal (coarse stone on emulsion) topped with a slurry seal layer.
Layer Thickness 3 mm to 6 mm (Thin surfacing) 10 mm to 15 mm (Medium-thick matrix)
Structural Strength Relies on binder/fine aggregate density; lower shear resistance under heavy turning. High tensile strength provided by embedded stone skeleton matrix; excellent resistance to turning shear.
Surface Texture & Skid Resistance Smooth, uniform, sand-like finish. Extremely quiet under traffic; standard traction. Macro-textured skid resistance of a chip seal combined with the smooth, flexible finish of slurry (no loose stones).
Expected Lifespan 5 to 7 Years 10 to 15 Years
Ideal Estate Usage Low-volume straight runs, light residential streets, preventative binder sealing (e.g., Duiker Ridge, Bridge 3 & 7). High-wear corridors, steep inclines, sweeping curves, and oxidized main arteries (e.g., Champagne Ridge, Oribi Lane).

Appendix A3: Granular Material Classifications (G1–G10)

Technical Reference — TRH14 / COLTO Standards

Standard South African pavement material classifications according to TRH14/COLTO specifications. Structural selection dictates load-bearing capacity, shear strength, and compaction requirements for estate road sub-bases.

Class Material Description Key Physical Requirements Primary Pavement Application
G1 Dense-graded crushed stone (High Shear Strength) 100% crushed rock; Parent rock UCS > 120 MPa; Compacted to 86–88% Apparent Relative Density (ARD). Heavy-duty base courses subject to extreme wheel loads and high traffic volumes.
G2 Dense-graded crushed stone (Standard Structural Base) Crushed gravel/rock; Max aggregate size 37.5mm; Compacted to 100–102% Mod AASHTO density. Primary structural base layer for high-wear estate corridors, paver bed box-cuts, and junction aprons.
G3 Crushed stone / Selected natural gravel mix At least 50% crushed particles; Compacted to 98–100% Mod AASHTO; CBR > 80%. Base layer for light-to-medium traffic roads or sub-base for heavy loading.
G4 Natural gravel (High Quality) Maximum PI < 6; CBR ≥ 80% at 98% Mod AASHTO compaction; Max aggregate size 53mm. Standard upper sub-base layer under G2/G3 base courses or paving blocks.
G5 Natural gravel (Medium Quality) Maximum PI < 10; CBR ≥ 45% at 95% Mod AASHTO compaction. Lower sub-base layer; washout excavation backfill and general sub-base stabilization.
G6 Natural gravel (Fair Quality) CBR ≥ 25% at 93% Mod AASHTO compaction; Plasticity Index (PI) < 12. Upper selected subgrade layer; slope leveling and deep void fill.
G7 Natural gravel / Coarse fill CBR ≥ 15% at 93% Mod AASHTO compaction; swell < 1.5%. Lower selected subgrade layer and embankment fill stabilization.
G8 Low-quality natural gravel / Fine soil CBR ≥ 10% at 90% Mod AASHTO compaction. General embankment foundation fill.
G9 Poor-quality soil / Clayey gravel CBR ≥ 7% at 90% Mod AASHTO compaction. Subgrade preparation and deep cut capping layers.
G10 Very poor / Expansive subgrade soil CBR < 3% at 90% Mod AASHTO compaction; high plasticity/swell potential. In-situ subgrade formation requiring undercut replacement or chemical stabilization.
