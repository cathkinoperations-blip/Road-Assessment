Appendix - Road Infrastructure Technical Specifications

Appendix A1: Structural Sub-Base Strategy

Technical Reference — Base Work Scope

A critical distinction in the master assessment plan is separating surface-level waterproofing from full structural sub-base reconstruction. Surface treatments do not automatically require redoing the sub-base, preserving capital where structural integrity remains intact.

1. Surface Sealing Only

Sub-base Left Intact

  • Mechanism: Applied directly onto existing asphalt.
  • Purpose: Waterproofs the surface, seals micro-cracks, and prevents binder oxidation.
  • Applicable Conditions: Fine map cracking, surface weathering, pinhole loss.
  • Target Corridors: Duiker Ridge, Village 4 & 5, Bridge 3 & 7 approaches.

2. Targeted Box Cuts

Localized Base Repairs

  • Mechanism: Cold-milling or hand-excavating localized failure zones down to sub-base.
  • Purpose: Rebuilds isolated sub-base collapse before applying a continuous surface seal.
  • Applicable Conditions: Wheel-path alligator cracking, isolated depressions, sunken patches.
  • Target Corridors: Bridge 5, Champagne Ridge, Eland Way, Oribi Lane.

3. Full Structural Rebuild

Full Depth Excavation Required

  • Mechanism: Total removal of failed sub-base; installation of concrete beams, drainage, or pavers.
  • Purpose: Re-establishes structural load-bearing capacity and edge control.
  • Applicable Conditions: Severe washout trenches, edge scour, high turning shear.
  • Target Corridors: Bridge 6 & 8 washout zones, Arum Lane apron.

Appendix A2: Bituminous Material Comparison

Technical Reference — Cape Seal vs. Slurry Overlay
Parameter Slurry Overlay (Slurry Seal) Cape Seal
System Construction Single-layer pre-mixture of bitumen emulsion, fine aggregate (sand/small stone), water, and cement filler. Two-step hybrid treatment: Single chip seal (coarse stone on emulsion) topped with a slurry seal layer.
Layer Thickness 3 mm to 6 mm (Thin surfacing) 10 mm to 15 mm (Medium-thick matrix)
Structural Strength Relies on binder/fine aggregate density; lower shear resistance under heavy turning. High tensile strength provided by embedded stone skeleton matrix; excellent resistance to turning shear.
Surface Texture & Skid Resistance Smooth, uniform, sand-like finish. Extremely quiet under traffic; standard traction. Macro-textured skid resistance of a chip seal combined with the smooth, flexible finish of slurry (no loose stones).
Expected Lifespan 5 to 7 Years 10 to 15 Years
Ideal Estate Usage Low-volume straight runs, light residential streets, preventative binder sealing (e.g., Duiker Ridge, Bridge 3/5/7). High-wear corridors, steep inclines, sweeping curves, and oxidized main arteries (e.g., Champagne Ridge, Oribi Lane).
