Homeowner Communication Strategy & Protocol

  • Early Aesthetic Consultation (September 2026 - Physical Townhall & Email Briefing): Scheduled *before* final commitments are locked in. Focused specifically on the strategic shift to interlocking pavers, addressing how the material choice impacts the estate's look and feel, long-term asset value, and drainage performance compared to alternative options[cite: 1].
  • Board Approval Sign-off (Late November 2026 - Formal Submission/Email): Presenting the finalized contractor quotes, material hedging plans, and financial breakdown to the board for formal sign-off[cite: 1].
  • Pre-Construction Operational Briefing (February 2027 - Physical/Virtual Townhall Meeting): Comprehensive overview for plot owners outlining expected construction impacts, timelines, alternate routes for Tier 1 areas (like Champagne Ridge and Bridges 6 & 8), and safety protocols[cite: 1].
  • Active Construction Updates (March – April 2027 - Monthly Email Digests & Flash Notices): Regular progress reports paired with targeted traffic advisories regarding temporary road closures or single-lane restrictions during active paving[cite: 1].
