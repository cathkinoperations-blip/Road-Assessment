Bridge 3 Series

Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (~35m Scope) — Tier 2 High Priority

Key Observations & Issues

  • Curved Approach Stress: Surface wear and binder loss accelerated along the bend leading into the stone parapets due to dynamic turning vehicle loads[cite: 13].
  • Localized Alligatoring: 5–8m zones showing fine, interconnected block/alligator cracking across the travel lane[cite: 13].
  • Sound Structural Foundation: Sub-base remains structurally aligned with no major structural slumping yet, making early intervention critical[cite: 13].

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • Preventative Crack Sealing: Rout out, air-blow clean, and fill all fine alligator and block cracks with hot polymer-modified bituminous sealant[cite: 13].
  • Rejuvenating Slurry Seal: Apply a dense bitumen slurry seal coat over the full 35m approach to restore the binder matrix and lock aggregate[cite: 13].
  • Side Channel & Shoulder Inspection: Clear shoulder margins leading into stone parapets to ensure stormwater efficiently runs off pavement edges into side drainage channels[cite: 13].
