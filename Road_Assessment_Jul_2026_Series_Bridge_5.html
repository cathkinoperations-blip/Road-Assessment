Bridge 5 Series

Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (~76m Scope) — Tier 2 High Priority

Key Observations & Issues

  • Mid-Span Base Settlement (15m Failure Zone): Heavy wheel-path fatigue alligator cracking and sag caused by sub-grade moisture saturation at the mid-span approach.
  • Culvert Inlet Wingwall Scour: Concentrated surface water running uncontained along verges, eroding soil support beside wingwall footings.
  • Corridor Binder Oxidation (60m Approach): Progressive binder aging and fine aggregate stripping along the broader approach requiring low-cost holding maintenance.

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • 15m G2 Rebuild & 80mm Paver Overlay: Excavate 15m mid-span settlement zone down 200mm, backfill with compacted G2 base aggregate, and lay heavy-duty 80mm interlocking pavers.
  • Wingwall V-Drain Protection: Construct a cast concrete V-drain transition apron to securely channel approach runoff into the culvert mouth without scouring wingwalls.
  • Corridor Holding Repairs: Execute targeted cold-mix leveling on minor dips and perform hot-pour crack sealing across the remaining 60m approach corridor.
