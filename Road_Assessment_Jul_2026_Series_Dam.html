Cathkin Dam Series

Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (~103m Scope) — Tier 2 High Priority

Key Observations & Issues

  • Embankment Edge Instability: Outer road edge showing active slumping and wheel-path fatigue cracking due to reservoir embankment proximity and trapped sub-surface moisture.
  • Retaining Wall Water Trapping & Shoving: Asphalt on the 28m dam bridge section exhibits severe upheaval, cracking, and sub-base displacement caused by water constrained against the stone wall.
  • Paver Transition & Speed Hump Shear: Cold-joint breakdown where interlocking pavers meet asphalt, along with displaced block pavers on the integrated speed hump.
  • Spillway Overflow Erosion: Uncontrolled flood-stage runoff crossing the road surface near the spillway intake causing binder stripping and aggregate loss.

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • Sub-surface Agricultural Drainage: Install subsurface agricultural sub-soil drains along the embankment shoulder to capture and divert ground moisture away from the roadbed.
  • Bridge Deck Sub-Base Undercut & Paver Rebuild: Excavate failed asphalt along the 28m retaining wall, re-compact sub-base with G2 base material wrapped in geotextile, and pave with 80mm interlocking pavers.
  • Speed Hump & Transition Reset: Reset displaced interlocking pavers on the speed hump, construct reinforced concrete header beams at paver-to-asphalt transitions.
  • Reinforced Concrete Overflow Apron: Construct a reinforced 25MPa concrete surface apron across the spillway crossing to manage high-velocity discharge without eroding pavement.
