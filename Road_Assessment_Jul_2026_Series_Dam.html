Cathkin Dam Series

Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (~75m Scope) — Tier 2 High Priority

Key Observations & Issues

  • Embankment Edge Instability: Outer road edge showing active slumping and wheel-path cracking due to reservoir embankment proximity and high soil moisture.
  • Spillway Overflow Erosion: Uncontrolled flood-stage runoff crossing the road surface near the dam overflow zone causes severe binder stripping and aggregate loss.
  • Sub-surface Water Piping: Saturated sub-grade conditions creating soft spots, piping voids, and localized road deck depressions.

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • Sub-surface Agricultural Drainage: Install subsurface agricultural sub-soil drains along the embankment shoulder to capture and divert ground moisture away from the roadbed.
  • Reinforced Concrete Overflow Apron: Construct a reinforced 25MPa concrete surface apron across the spillway crossing to manage high-velocity discharge without eroding pavement.
  • Sub-Base Re-compaction & Repair: Execute deep box-cut undercuts on depressed zones, backfill with compacted G2 base material wrapped in geotextile fabric, and seal joints.
