Village 5 Series

Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (~16m Scope) — Tier 3 Medium-High Priority

Key Observations & Issues

  • Wheel-Path Fatigue Alligatoring: Concentrated network of crocodile cracking running along the main wheel track over a 16m stretch.
  • Oxidized Binder Layer: Surface binder matrix is depleted, allowing rainwater to pass directly into the underlying sub-base.
  • Incipient Sub-Base Weakening: Moisture infiltration starting to soften top sub-base fines.

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • High-Pressure Crack Sealing: Route out, air-blow clean, and fill all primary wheel-path crack networks with hot polymer-modified bituminous sealant.
  • Full-Width Slurry Overlay: Apply a dense slurry coat across the 16m approach to lock in loose aggregate and restore full surface waterproofing.
  • Edge Joint Cleanout: Trim shoulder margins and blow out edge joints prior to slurry coat application to ensure proper seal bonding.
