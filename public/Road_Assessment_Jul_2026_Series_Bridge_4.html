Bridge 4 Series

Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (~195m Scope) — Tier 2 High Priority

Key Observations & Issues

  • Trapped Deck Moisture (30m Deck Zone): Water pooling between stone parapet walls due to inadequate drainage outlets, resulting in deep deck potholing and base softening.
  • Culvert Inlet Scour: High-velocity surface water bypassing inlets and scouring uncontained shoulder slopes adjacent to wingwalls.
  • Corridor Extension Aging (100m Scope): Binder oxidation and fine block cracking along the extended approach road requiring low-cost holding intervention.

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • 30m Paver Deck Rebuild & Weep Channels: Rebuild 30m deck section with heavy-duty 80mm interlocking pavers and install 150mm concrete weep channels for continuous drainage through parapets.
  • Culvert Inlet Armoring: Construct stone-pitched channel aprons at culvert entries to safely control and discharge stormwater velocity.
  • 100m Extension Holding Repair: Perform targeted spot patching on localized potholes and execute hot-pour crack sealing along the remaining 100m asphalt extension.
