📋 Core Project & Meeting Materials

  • Road Upgrade Kick-Off Presentation

    This presentation sets out the strategic shift away from temporary cosmetic patching toward a hybrid rigid-pavement engineering model across the estate:

    • Core Recommendation: Implement a hybrid surface strategy combining 60mm (35 MPa) Interlocking Pavers for standard flat corridors with 150mm Reinforced Cast Concrete (30 MPa) on steep inclines and water channels.
    • Structural Superiority: Flexible bituminous seals split under heavy 10-cube truck axle loads (~8.2 t) due to zero bridging strength. Rigid pavers bridge underlying subgrade movements without cracking.
    • Zero Access Delays: Paver installations allow cars to drive over laid sections immediately, avoiding access disruptions in single-road estate zones.
    • 20-Year TCO Leader: Eliminates recurring re-sealing cycles, offering the lowest 20-year Total Cost of Ownership (R500–R750/m²) with 100% reusable blocks during subgrade repairs.
    • Execution Standard: 90° Herringbone laying pattern over a 20–25mm washed river sand bed, restrained laterally by in-situ concrete edge kerbs and swept with silica joint sand.

    Serving as the technical engineering reference for the assessment, this appendix details structural sub-base strategies, material standards, and regional benchmarks:

    • Sub-Base Strategy Matrix: Differentiates non-invasive surface waterproofing (preserves capital on sound beds) from targeted box-cut repairs and total structural rebuilds (G2 aggregate undercutting & edge restraints).

    • Regional Logistics Benchmarking: Contrasts urban-adjacent developments (e.g., Amber Valley) with Cathkin Estates' remote mountain setting, justifying self-sufficient "lift-and-relay" interlocking pavers over high-risk ready-mix concrete transit.
