📋 Core Project & Meeting Materials

  • Road Upgrade Kick-Off Presentation

    This presentation sets out the strategic shift away from temporary cosmetic patching toward a hybrid rigid-pavement engineering model across the estate:

    • Core Recommendation: Implement a hybrid surface strategy combining 60mm (35 MPa) Interlocking Pavers for standard flat corridors with 150mm Reinforced Cast Concrete (30 MPa) on steep inclines and water channels[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span)[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).
    • Structural Superiority: Flexible bituminous seals split under heavy 10-cube truck axle loads (~8.2 t) due to zero bridging strength[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span)[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span). Rigid pavers bridge underlying subgrade movements without cracking[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).
    • Zero Access Delays: Paver installations allow cars to drive over laid sections immediately, avoiding access disruptions in single-road estate zones[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span)[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).
    • 20-Year TCO Leader: Eliminates recurring re-sealing cycles, offering the lowest 20-year Total Cost of Ownership (R500–R750/m²) with 100% reusable blocks during subgrade repairs[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
    • Execution Standard: 90° Herringbone laying pattern over a 20–25mm washed river sand bed, restrained laterally by in-situ concrete edge kerbs and swept with silica joint sand[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span)[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).

  • Assessment Portal

    Serving as the central navigation hub for the July 2026 infrastructure review, this portal integrates master executive summary decks with granular, tier-based corridor breakdowns and spatial visualisations[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span):

    • Scope & Strategy: Categorises 18 distinct estate road corridors (~1,595m) into a four-tier priority matrix, balancing permanent paver/concrete rebuilds on critical failure zones with cost-effective holding fixes on recoverable asphalt[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span).
    • Tier 1 — Critical Priority: Focuses on permanent rebuilds for severe oxidation, washouts, and structural failures across Champagne Ridge (~280m), Bridge 6 Series (~60m), and Bridge 8 Series (~140m)[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).
    • Tier 2 — High Priority: Addresses base and drainage failures including bridge approaches (Bridge 2, 4, 5), Arum Lane apron (~36m paver apron), and Cathkin Drive Dam Series (~103m slumping, piping cavitation & sub-soil drainage)[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span).
    • Tier 3 & 4 — Medium/Low Priority: Outlines low-cost, targeted maintenance (hot-pour crack sealing, mastic patching, cold-mix leveling) across residential lanes (Eland Way, Dierama Lane, Oribi Lane, Whydah Way) and low-wear bridge approaches[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span).
    • Interactive Spatial Mapping: Features an embedded Google GIS network map allowing interactive layer toggling to inspect damage logs and repair specifications for individual road segments[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span).
