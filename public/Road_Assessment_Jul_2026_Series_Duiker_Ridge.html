Duiker Ridge

Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (63m Scope) — Tier 3 Medium-High Priority

Key Observations & Issues

  • Binder Stripping on Bend (18m): Extreme surface coarseness and aggregate exposure where bitumen has oxidized under turning vehicle shear.
  • Parallel Shoulder Crack (45m): Continuous linear longitudinal crack running parallel to the uncurbed grass shoulder edge allowing water infiltration.
  • Stable Sub-Base Foundation: Sub-base layers remain firm and intact with zero deep structural potholes or sub-grade failure present.

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • Elastomeric Crack Sealing (45m): Route out, clean, and hot-pour seal the continuous 45-meter parallel shoulder crack with elastomeric bitumen.
  • Full-Width Slurry Rejuvenation (63m Total Scope): Apply a dense bitumen slurry seal coat across the entire 63m corridor to re-bind loose aggregate on the bend and fully seal the surface against water ingress.
  • Shoulder Edge Trimming: Trim back grass encroachment along shoulder margins prior to slurry application to ensure clean perimeter adhesion.
