Eland Way

Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (90m Corridor Scope) — Tier 3 Medium-High Priority

Key Observations & Issues

  • Persistent Centerline Fracture (90m): Continuous longitudinal cracking stretching down the center of the 90-meter downhill corridor.
  • 10–15m Centerline Crocodile Cracking: Advanced localized alligatoring along the center travel line indicating top-down fatigue failure and sub-base moisture softening.
  • Failed Historical Patches: Depressed historical patch zones on the slope with perimeter joint failure funneling stormwater runoff into base layers.

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • Route & Crack Seal (90m Corridor): Rout out the continuous 90m centerline longitudinal crack, clean with compressed air, and fill with elastomeric hot-pour bitumen sealant.
  • G2 Box-Cut Undercut: Excavate the 10–15m centerline alligatoring zone, backfill with compacted G2 crushed stone aggregate to restore sub-base structural integrity.
  • Patch Re-Excavation & Leveling: Re-excavate sunken historical patch zones down to sub-base, re-compact base layers, and lay level asphalt patches to prevent water pooling on the slope.
