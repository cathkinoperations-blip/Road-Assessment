3b. Technical Evaluation & Engineering Assessment

  • Material Specifications: Proposes 80mm Interlocking Pavers Grey - Class 30/2.0 s-a (Corobrick's), suitable for heavy traffic as per ASTM C936 standards.
  • Methodology: Paving overlaying existing asphalt surface, including 25mm sand bedding and FIG 8 curbs.
  • Work Diagram Integrity: Standard structural cross-section provided (compacted soil subgrade, geotextile, compacted aggregate/stabilized base, bedding sand, pavers with sand-filled joints, edge restraint/curb).
  • Engineering Risk: Overlaying existing asphalt without explicit milling or sub-base geotechnical testing data introduces risk of reflective cracking or sub-base failure if existing asphalt is compromised.

3c. Commercial and Contractual Assessment

  • High Cash-Flow Risk: Demanding an 80% upfront deposit and only 20% on completion exposes participants to severe financial risk and contractor default. Standard commercial practice typically requires maximum 20-30% deposit with milestone/retention structures.
  • Retention Restrictions: Clause explicitly states "No retention money is to be held unless agreed in writing" — highly disadvantageous given the lack of defect liability withholding.
  • Warranty: Offers a 36-month project guarantee, which is favorable.
  • Ownership Retention: Materials remain the property of JB Construction until paid in full (standard, but tied to heavy deposit terms).

4. Key Exclusions & Fine Print Caveats

  • All prices exclude VAT (Value Added Tax).
  • Prices are subject to exchange rate fluctuations.
  • Timeline estimated at 30 to 60 working days, subject to weather and timely payments.
  • Site will be left tidy and rubble removed "unless otherwise discussed".

5. Discrepancies & Audit Findings

⚠️ Scope Ambiguity: The total quoted price is R2,366,000.00 with a stated unit rate of R826.70 / m². Mathematically, this dictates a project size of ~2,862 m². If evaluating for an 810 m² scope, the lump sum must be clarified.
⚠️ Registration / Legal Discrepancy: The company registration number on page 1 is listed as 2025 / 551825 / 07 (indicating a future-dated or placeholder CIPC registration year of 2025, while proposal dates are in August 2026), warranting formal verification of corporate legal standing.
⚠️ Contact Information Discrepancy: Operations Manager Addie Mpofu provides a personal Gmail address (ediempofu@gmail.com) alongside a corporate email (Addie.Mpofu@jobmanconstruction.co.za), which reduces institutional professionalism.
