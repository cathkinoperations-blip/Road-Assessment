3. Technical Evaluation & Engineering Assessment

Sub-base Suitability: Laying interlocking pavers directly on an existing tar road using a river sand bedding layer requires careful evaluation. If the existing asphalt has structural failures, cracking, or severe rutting, reflection cracking or localized settling may occur without milling or a leveling layer.

Kerb Installation: Excavating and concreting footings for Figure 10 kerbs is standard practice, but drainage gradients and tie-ins with existing stormwater infrastructure are not explicitly detailed in the scope.

Material Specs: The quotation lacks precise specifications regarding the paver strength (e.g., 25MPa or 30MPa heavy-duty traffic ratings) and edge restraints beyond the Figure 10 kerbs.

4. Commercial & Contractual Terms Assessment

Risk Allocation: The contract terms are informal. There are no clauses defining retention money, defect liability periods (warranty), penalties for delay (liquidated damages), or escalation terms.

Payment Schedule: No milestones or deposit structures are defined. A formal contract must outline progress payment terms to mitigate financial exposure.

5. Key Exclusions & Fine Print Caveats

  • Value Added Tax (VAT) is added separately; quote states totals are "plus vat".
  • No mention of geotechnical testing or sub-base repair contingencies if bad underlying material is exposed during kerb excavation.
  • Excludes potential municipal permits, traffic accommodation/diversion costs during construction, and waste disposal fees.

6. Discrepancies & Audit Findings

  • Area Discrepancy: The request/baseline assumes an 810m² area, whereas the supplier's quote explicitly prices for 800m² (R441,600.00). This requires a commercial adjustment (pro-rated increase of R5,520.00 ex VAT).
  • Lack of Formal Validity: The quotation does not state a validity period, leaving pricing vulnerable to material cost fluctuations (cement, aggregates, concrete blocks).
  • Missing Compliance Documentation: No proof of CIDB grading, Letter of Good Standing (COIDA), or tax clearance certificates are attached to the proposal.
