Technical Evaluation & Engineering Assessment

  • Kerb Foundation Spec: The proposal specifies a 50mm thick concrete foundation for Fig 10 Kerbs. Engineering review should verify if 50mm provides adequate structural support against vehicular loads, as standard specifications often call for a minimum of 75mm–100mm unreinforced concrete haunching/base.
  • Paver Structural Profile: 80mm G-Block pavers are specified, which are well-suited for medium-to-heavy vehicular traffic expected on estate roads.
  • Bedding & Jointing: 20mm sand bedding with sand grouting is standard, though long-term performance will depend heavily on sub-grade compaction (not explicitly detailed in the scope).
  • Measurement Methodology: Note 4 dictates that areas will be measured inclusive of wastage due to edge cutting. This is standard practice but increases the material quantity exposure per net square meter laid.

Execution & Technical Terms Assessment

  • Scope Boundaries: Note 2 dictates that any "remedial work" must be priced separately prior to commencement. Engineers should audit site conditions beforehand to define baseline sub-grade readiness.
  • Quantity Verification: Note 3 establishes that all areas are subject to re-measurement upon completion, meaning final quantities may vary from the original estimate.

Key Technical Exclusions & Scope Caveats

  • Remedial/prep work excluded from current baseline and subject to separate ad-hoc scope definition.
  • Final billing subject strictly to re-measurement of installed areas on completion.
  • Wastage for edge-cutting is included directly into the measured areas.

Engineering & Audit Findings

  • Omission of Execution Parameters: The quote omits project timeline/duration, quality compliance milestones, and site handover specifications.
  • Geotechnical Risk: No mention of sub-base stabilization or compaction testing certificates, leaving potential exposure to structural failure if existing road bases are sub-standard.
