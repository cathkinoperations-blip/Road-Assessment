Engineering Evaluation Questionnaire

Technical Review Questions for Estate Paving & Kerbing Proposal

1 Sub-Base & Geotechnical Integrity

  • Existing Road Base Condition: Does the existing road bed require sub-grade stabilization, box-cut, or imported sub-base material prior to paving, or is the current surface ready for sand bedding? [1]
  • Compaction Testing: What degree of compaction (e.g., MOD AASHTO density) is required for the sub-base, and should compaction testing certificates be made a mandatory prerequisite before laying pavers? [1]
  • Remedial Work Risk: Since remedial work is excluded from the vendor's baseline quote, what specific preliminary preparation or repair work will be necessary before contractor site establishment? [1], [2]

2 Kerb Foundation & Structural Edge Restraint

  • Kerb Concrete Bedding Thickness: The proposal specifies a 50mm concrete foundation for the Fig 10 kerbs. Is 50mm adequate for expected estate traffic loads, or should this be increased to standard 75mm–100mm haunching to prevent kerb displacement or cracking? [1], [2]
  • Concrete Specification: What concrete strength class (e.g., 20 MPa or 25 MPa) should be specified for the kerb bedding and backing? [1], [2]

3 Surface Specifications & Load Bearing

  • Paver Load Rating: Are 80mm G-Block pavers sufficient for all expected vehicle types (including heavy delivery trucks, refuse vehicles, and emergency equipment) accessing these estate roads? [1], [2]
  • Sand Bedding & Jointing Sand: Is 20mm bedding sand appropriate, and should a specific silica/plaster sand or stabilizer be specified for joint filling to prevent sand washing out on slopes or under heavy rain? [1], [2]
  • Drainage & Stormwater: How will surface water runoff be handled, and do the proposed pavement levels tie in correctly with existing stormwater channels and kerb discharge points? [2]

4 Quantity Measurement & Wastage

  • Net vs. Gross Measurement: The quote states that final areas will be measured inclusive of cutting wastage. What is the estimated wastage percentage for this specific road layout, and should we require field measurements to be based on net laid area instead? [1], [2]
  • As-Built Re-measurement: What process will the Engineer use to conduct joint on-site re-measurements to verify the final line, level, and area before approving final payment? [1], [2]

5 Quality Control & Project Timeline

  • Execution Timeline: What is a realistic construction timeline for 810m² of paving and 540m of kerbing, and what key quality checkpoints or hold points should be established during execution? [1], [2]
  • Warranty & Defect Liability: What defect liability period (e.g., 6 or 12 months) should be imposed on the contractor for issues like settlement, rutting, or loose pavers? [1]
