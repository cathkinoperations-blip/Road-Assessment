Appendix A: Prioritization Framework
Criteria Matrix for Road Infrastructure Investment
 Technical Appendix
1. Structural Integrity 30%

Core Question: Is the underlying foundation/sub-base still sound, or is it actively eroding or failing?

Critical Threshold:

Sub-base washout, active mud pumping, edge scour trenches, subterranean voids, or total base collapse.

Preventative Threshold:

Intact sub-base with only surface binder oxidation or light weathering.
2. Hydraulic Exposure 25%

Core Question: Is water actively entering structural layers or undermining bridge deck/headwall footings?

Critical Threshold:

Trapped water against parapets, unsealed cracks on steep inclines, missing side drains, or edge scour.

Preventative Threshold:

Natural water shedding with functioning side drains and intact kerbs.
3. Progression Speed 20%

Core Question: How fast will this failure escalate during the next wet season if left unaddressed?

Critical Threshold:

Exponential deterioration (e.g., open cracks near river crossings or steep downhills turning into blowouts).

Preventative Threshold:

Linear, slow-moving deterioration (e.g., surface aggregate stripping).
4. Traffic & Stress Load 20%

Core Question: Is this section subjected to heavy dynamic loads, turning shear, or high traffic volumes?

Critical Threshold:

Main access corridors, heavy turning aprons/intersections, steep gradient approaches, or bridge transitions.

Preventative Threshold:

Cul-de-sacs, quiet side lanes, or low-speed straightaways.

Core Question: What is the cost multiplier if we delay repairs by 12 months?

High Multiplier Impact:

Delaying a R10,000 crack seal today results in a R150,000 deep-lift base rebuild next year once water destroys the sub-base layer.

Low Multiplier Impact:

Delaying simple surface slurry coats on sound sub-bases has minimal impact on underlying asset value.
