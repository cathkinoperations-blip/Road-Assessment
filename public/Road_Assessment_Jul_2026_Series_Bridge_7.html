Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (~35m Scope) — Tier 2 High Priority

Key Observations & Issues

  • Incipient Wheel-Path Fatigue: Single prominent longitudinal crack developing along the outer wheel path on the curved approach[cite: 14].
  • Early Surface Aging: Minor localized alligatoring appearing where vehicles track around the bend approaching stone parapet walls[cite: 14].
  • Structurally Sound Base: Base course shows no signs of sub-grade slumping, mud pumping, or severe potholing[cite: 14].

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • Targeted Crack Sealing: Rout out, air-blow clean, and hot-pour seal the linear longitudinal crack with polymer bitumen before wet weather worsens flexure[cite: 14].
  • Surface Rejuvenation: Apply a single-layer slurry or Cape Seal across the ~35m curved section to protect against binder drying and lock aggregate[cite: 14].
  • Drainage Runoff Alignment: Inspect shoulder margins leading to bridge walls to ensure unhindered stormwater shedding[cite: 14].
