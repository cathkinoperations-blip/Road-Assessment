Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (~51m Scope) — Tier 4 Low Priority

Key Observations & Issues

  • Early Binder Degradation: Sun exposure and age have oxidized the surface bitumen across a combined ~51m length, resulting in aggregate dryness.
  • Minor Hair-Line Cracking: Fine longitudinal cracks are beginning to form along wheel paths on the 17m curved section.
  • Structurally Sound Base: No sub-base failure, rutting, edge break, or washout observed along either section.

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • High-Pressure Air Blow-Out: Pressure clean all active micro-cracks along the 17m section to remove grit and loose aggregate.
  • Hot-Pour Elastomeric Crack Sealing: Inject hot rubberized bitumen sealant into longitudinal cracks along the 17m section to halt water ingress.
  • Preventative Rejuvenation / Band Sealing: Apply localized bitumen emulsion band-sealing over stripped areas across the 34m stretch to bind aggregate and delay resurfacing needs.
