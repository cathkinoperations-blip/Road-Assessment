Village 4 Series

Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (~70m Scope) — Tier 3 Medium-High Priority

Key Observations & Issues

  • Full-Width Map Cracking: Widespread micro-cracking driven by surface binder oxidation and solar exposure across the main ~40m section[cite: 12].
  • Traffic Calming Stress Zone: Interconnected block cracking clustered on the approach to the paved speed hump threshold due to dynamic braking/accelerating shear forces[cite: 12].
  • Stable Base Alignment: Underlying sub-base layers remain firm and structurally aligned with zero sub-grade failures present[cite: 12].

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • Transition Crack Sealing: Rout out, air-blow clean, and hot-pour seal all active cracks near the speed hump transition interface with rubberized bitumen[cite: 12].
  • Full-Corridor Slurry Rejuvenation: Apply a dense full-width bitumen slurry seal coat across the entire ~70m corridor scope to seal micro-cracks and prevent water infiltration into base layers[cite: 12].
  • Threshold Interface Leveling: Edge-trim and level transition margins where asphalt meets the rigid speed hump structure prior to sealing[cite: 12].
