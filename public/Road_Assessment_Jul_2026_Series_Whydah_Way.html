Whydah Way Series

Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (~110m Scope) — Tier 3 Medium Priority

Key Observations & Issues

  • Incline Wheel-Path Fatigue: Uphill braking and accelerating forces have driven longitudinal cracking and localized alligatoring along the 38m stretch.
  • Localized Pothole Delamination: Concentrated wearing course breakdown in wheel tracks leading to water penetration into the sub-grade.
  • Uncontained Shoulder Margins: Stormwater running off the incline has eroded asphalt edges, leaving uncontained margins susceptible to progressive edge break.
  • Speed Hump Transition: Cracking and joint gap forming where asphalt meets the speed hump structure.

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • Square-Cut Pothole Patching: Excavate localized alligator/pothole fatigue zones (Detail area) to sub-base level, backfill with G2 aggregate, and apply dense cold/hot-mix asphalt patch.
  • Hot-Pour Crack Sealing: High-pressure clean and seal all longitudinal and block cracks along the 38m incline stretch using rubberized elastomeric bitumens.
  • Speed Hump Joint Repair: Rout and pour elastomeric joint sealant along the speed hump contact seam to block water infiltration.
  • Shoulder Margin Stabilization: Trim eroded edges and backfill shoulder margins with compacted soil/G5 gravel to prevent lateral edge collapse.
