Bridge 8 Series

Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (~140m Scope) — Priority 3 Critical

Key Observations & Issues

  • 59m Steep Incline Fracture: Continuous longitudinal shear fracture along the steep approach slope caused by traffic torque and lateral embankment displacement[cite: 10].
  • Abutment Approach Separation: Severe differential settlement and joint faulting where flexible surfacing meets the rigid bridge deck structure[cite: 10].
  • Headwall Shoulder Erosion: High-velocity surface runoff bypassing inlets, resulting in severe embankment washouts and shoulder collapse near wingwalls[cite: 10].

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • Embankment Armor & Concrete Edge Curbs: Install heavy stone rip-rap slope stabilization and cast concrete edge curbs/chutes at wingwalls to channel water safely into culverts[cite: 10].
  • Concrete Transition Apron: Cast a reinforced concrete transition apron across the bridge approach with elastomeric expansion joint seals[cite: 10].
  • 80mm Interlocking Paver Rebuild: Excavate 59m fracture zone, re-compact G2 sub-base aggregate, install concrete edge restraints, and lay 80mm Class 40 interlocking pavers for permanent shear resistance[cite: 10].
