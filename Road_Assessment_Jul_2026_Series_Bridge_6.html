Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (~60m Scope) — Priority 2 Critical

Key Observations & Issues

  • Critical Parapet Edge Washout: Direct stormwater channeling along the stone parapet wall footing has scoured out the sub-base, creating an active undercut trench[cite: 9].
  • Historical Patch Joint Failure: Severe perimeter joint breakdown on old asphalt patches allowing continuous water infiltration into the road bed[cite: 9].
  • Complete Surface Loss: Multiple localized zones exhibit complete binder stripping down to uncontained sub-base aggregate[cite: 9].

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • Concrete Edge Curbs: Install robust concrete edge curbs along the parapet wall footing to contain the outer edges and prevent lateral shoulder scour[cite: 9].
  • Sub-Base Undercut & Backfill: Excavate failed historic patches and scoured edge zones, backfill with compacted G2 crushed stone aggregate to 98% Mod AASHTO[cite: 9].
  • Interlocking Paver Overlay: Lay 80mm interlocking pavers on bedding sand across the ~60m stretch, tied firmly into the concrete edge restraints[cite: 9].
