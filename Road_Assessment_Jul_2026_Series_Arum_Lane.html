Arum Lane Series

Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (~65m Scope) — Tier 3 Medium-High Priority

Key Observations & Issues

  • Junction Turning Shear: Severe surface breakup at the Cathkin Drive junction apron caused by vehicle turning torque on weak asphalt margins.
  • Alligator Fatigue Outside #52: Localized sub-base weakening resulting in wheel-path alligator cracking and potholing in front of property #52.
  • Longitudinal Joint Separation: Continuous centerline thermal separation allowing stormwater penetration into underlying sub-base layers.

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • Paver Apron Installation: Construct an 80mm heavy-duty interlocking paver apron at the Cathkin Drive turning junction to permanently handle high turning torque.
  • Targeted G2 Holding Patch: Execute a G2 base box-cut undercut and asphalt patch outside #52 to stabilize localized fatigue failure.
  • Centerline Crack Sealing: Route, clean, and seal all longitudinal centerline cracks across the remaining ~65m corridor with hot-pour rubberized bitumen.
