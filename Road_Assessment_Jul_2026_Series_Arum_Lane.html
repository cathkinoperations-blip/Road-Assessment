Arum Lane Apron

Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (~15m Scope) — Tier 2 High Priority

Key Observations & Issues

  • Junction Turning Shear: Concentrated surface breakdown at the Cathkin Drive turning apron caused by severe vehicle turning torque on brittle asphalt binder.
  • Sub-Base Moisture Penetration: Micro-alligatoring allowing surface runoff to seep into the sub-base layer directly adjacent to the main arterial road.

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • Heavy-Duty Paver Apron: Excavate failed ~15m asphalt apron section and install an 80mm Class 40 interlocking paver apron.
  • Concrete Edge Containment: Cast concrete edge restraint beams along the apron perimeter to lock pavers in place and prevent lateral spreading under heavy turn loads.
