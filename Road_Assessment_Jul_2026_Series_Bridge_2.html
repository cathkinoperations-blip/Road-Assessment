Bridge 2 Series

Corridor Summary & Fix Plan (~95m Scope) — Tier 2 High Priority

Key Observations & Issues

  • Delaminated Historic Patching: Extensive patch failures over the ~95m bridge deck and approach leading into stone parapet walls.
  • Uncontained Shoulder Edge: Severe shoulder gravel erosion along pavement margins letting water infiltrate sub-grade.
  • Centerline Crack Propagation: Longitudinal cracking extending down the center lane from flexure and heavy vehicle tracking.

Consolidated Fix Plan

  • Concrete Edge Containment: Cast continuous reinforced concrete edge beams along exposed road shoulders to prevent edge breakdown.
  • Interlocking Paver Conversion: Excavate failed asphalt patch zones, compact sub-base with G2 aggregate, and lay 80mm heavy-duty interlocking pavers across the ~95m bridge approach.
  • Deck Drainage Weep Channels: Install 150mm concrete drainage channels along parapet wall footings to channel runoff off the deck surface.
